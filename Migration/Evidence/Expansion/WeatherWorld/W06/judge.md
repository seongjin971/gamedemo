Rock profiles and conifer group proportions differ; the reference has a different balance of foreground mass and roadside enclosure. Abbey roof, wall and ruin proportions differ and the actual player occupies a slightly different position/scale in the corresponding view. HUD text is absent from the targets. Correcting these would require separately authorized scenery/camera work, not this weather pass.
- **Lighting:** Snow trees retain almost-black interiors and sharp separation from smoother brighter ground. The target's slate-blue midtones and distributed airborne snow integrate the layers more deeply. Large gusts need elongated, irregular breakup at several depths to approach the target, rather than rounded haze patches. Flash coverage now follows the user, but pale diffuse lifting lacks selective surface highlights and convincing retained contact shadows. The target's wet road has bright irregular glints, while the actual stones brighten relatively evenly. More gray overlay would not address that gap.
- **Materials:** Snow caps remain thick, smooth and rounded; rock undercuts are conspicuously dark. Conifer branches have repeated clumped shapes. The approach retains strongly separated bare road patches. Abbey roof tiles and masonry have simpler relief; the roof remains matte. Road stones lack the target's irregular wet roughness and micro-reflections. Tree trunks are smooth/pale, grass is wiry and sparse, and the surrounding soil has comparatively uniform texture.
- **Details:** The target contains much finer granular snow, bark/needle variation, wet contact detail, moss, litter and broken ground texture. Actual snow streaks remain more individually legible and similarly angled. Rain splash rings are isolated repeated motifs. Repeated stone patterns, vegetation shapes and HUD text leave substantial pixel-level differences.
