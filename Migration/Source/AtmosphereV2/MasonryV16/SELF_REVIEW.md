# Source prototype review: native comparison required

This package is ready for an actual Unity diagnostic comparison. It is not a convincing large visual advance, a finished masonry pass, or a scene-wide acceptance result.

The final full normal, clay and Rock05 material views, and all six matching material region renders, were opened directly. The normal/clay before images remain valid from the first batch because their untouched V13/V12 baseline and context are identical. Both final material before/after frames were rerendered with the same world-metre UV mapping on the compared objects. The small images are actual Blender border renders at the full camera's original pixel scale, not enlarged illustrations or retouched screenshots.

The new flight has more readable irregular face losses on five courses, unequal transverse stone widths, and stronger localized normal changes. This is easier to see in `stairs-material-before.png` versus `stairs-material-after.png` than in the whole frame. Nevertheless the ten horizontal riser bands remain dominant. Broad top rectangles and preserved five courses still read as manufactured. The prototype does not visibly establish that the Unity black-stripe blocker has cleared.

The new lower hero set puts damage in the main camera's lower portal and flame-pier region. Some face breaks and cap corners are visible. Existing thin vertical trim masks parts of the masonry and much of the pillar silhouette remains clean. The revised central bedding break aims at the exposed bay between trim strips, but eight clearly weathered hero faces are not yet a strong at-a-glance result in the full source image.

The right facade changes more clearly in its region pair: 63 small applied skins become 50 larger, unequal stone bodies, including two genuine double-height headers that terminate horizontal joints. The internal core shafts remain. This is a construction change, not merely another pair of normalized variants in the same slots. It still reads as a deliberately built wall, and the full-frame significance must be checked under native fog and lighting.

The first diagnostic pass used a broad single oblique cut. Its roughly 58 percent projected changed area overstated the visible improvement; most front plane angles were only around 8-11 degrees. It also failed seven nominal support ray samples, up to 3.88 mm behind the internal support front. That pass and its six images/failed report are preserved under `diagnostic-pass1/` and are not the final source.

The corrected source has central layered fracture bands with varying positions and a continuous depth compression that keeps the rear fixed. Actual triangle-normal samples across the entire new front surface have a median of 5.99 degrees and a 90th percentile of 31.40 degrees. The changed area is not uniformly a strong 14-25 degree face. Some boundaries are steeper, and much of the retained face is still near vertical. These measurements are diagnostic limits, not a visual score.

Final source validation passed: 45 untouched stair bodies preserve exact triangle-referenced positions, normals, UVs and RGBA; eight hero source identities and all 63 facade identities match the original bridge. Every exported body is closed with positive volume, finite attributes and positive winding against supplied normals. Repeating the triangle check after Unity float32 rounding yields no nonpositive dots or degeneracies. There are 71,736 stair triangles, 50,320 facade triangles and 7,902 triangles across the eight normalized hero replacements.

The final 12,240 first-hit support samples have zero violations and at least 8.05 mm nominal clearance in front of the retained support. This is a sampled source-box clearance result, not a native shadow test or a claim about every possible continuous contact point. The ten support boxes and core foundations are preserved.

The source scene holds lighting constant within comparisons but does not recreate the Unity HDR environment, wetness model, separate water, fire, character or non-instanced arch ring. Rock05 maps are reused unchanged from their recorded CC0 source; the added geometric pits and bedding breaks are authored shapes, not newly scanned physical measurements. PavingV15, TreeV12 and ContactV14 remain unchanged context. Native shadow/specular response, movement and performance still need root's separate validation. No new independent score or user adoption is assigned.
