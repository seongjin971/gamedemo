# Masonry V12 source self review

Status: ready for native material/lighting comparison. This is not scene Tier3 acceptance.

Directly reviewed the original concept, candidate36 full/zoom and verdict6 during diagnosis. Then directly inspected V8's original clay variants, every V12 variant view and the identical stacked-wall comparison. The V8 wall shows a broad repeated bright shoulder across almost every top edge, with deep dark belts under neighboring courses. The final V12 wall substantially reduces that repeated profile while retaining uneven face volumes, unequal corners and local fissures. The new wall's silhouette is more square and its mortar-sized gaps are thinner. Those observations support the intended bounded geometry experiment.

Two new-cube approaches were rejected: the first looked like almost perfect concrete blocks with small drilled holes; widening the cutters improved the holes but did not repair the overly pristine body. Both are preserved. A third approach derived directly from the V8 topology and compressed all near-plane geometry; that removed the shoulder but over-flattened the face centers. The delivered fourth pass uses strong perimeter compression and only 20% displacement at the central face, preserving V8's useful irregular body. Parent approval to test the current stable geometry was received after direct parent inspection of wall/variant images.

Remaining visual risks are explicit. The final stones still have a strong squared outline and some broad faces are quiet; these could read as new cut blocks under an unsuitable texture scale or smooth material. Some inherited long top fissures repeat across variants. The two large corner fractures are deliberately planar and may look too simple in close native views. This asset does not solve regular instance spacing, material color strips or perfectly aligned vertical courses. Root is changing those separately. Do not claim the banding problem fully solved until the actual portal/facade native pixels demonstrate it.

The eight exported meshes total 5,052 triangles. Independent validation measures strictly positive cross-dot summed corner normals for every triangle, zero degenerate triangles, finite arrays, valid indices, exact normalized bounds and unit normals. Blender seam-welded closure checks find zero nonmanifold edges. Original V8 file hashes remain unchanged. These checks establish a usable geometry candidate; they do not establish FPS, camera motion, final scene score or user adoption.

The repeated PowerShell exit 1 wrappers in scratch logs come from Blender's `Material.use_nodes` deprecation warning. The successful final log explicitly records all four renders, saved blend, `MASONRY_V12_COMPLETE 5052`, and normal Blender quit. Earlier failed boundary assertions and rejected visual attempts remain in their logs. No Unity file was edited or Editor launched by this subtask.
