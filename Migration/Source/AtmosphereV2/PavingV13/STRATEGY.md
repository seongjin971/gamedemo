# Paving V13 — neighboring source stones

Candidate45 full was opened directly. V12 improves local edges but its 24 raised bodies are visibly isolated within a much flatter bed. V13 therefore removes the sparse screen-space selection and repeats every traced source stone in its actual original photo location across the front courtyard footprint. It clips bodies at the existing front rectangle, preserves the masked rear bed and the staircase/landing scope, and retains the original 9.2 × 4.6 m art-scale UV period.

Forty dominant source contours cover only 52.3% of the photo tile before any bevel. Root approved adding neighboring medium units to avoid replacing those stones with false giant contours. The current seed file contains 40 dominant and 52 subordinate photographic regions, occupying 68.38% of the source image. These are manually traced against actual Color/AO, followed by bounded AO/height-gradient edge refinement; this is not a claim of automatic semantic segmentation. No random UV offset or shuffled source texture is introduced.

Coverage reporting distinguishes the broad top surfaces including their sloped arrises from the flat inner cap alone. The former should occupy 60–75% of the foreground. The latter is necessarily smaller because real bevels consume projected surface area. Both measurements are exported, rather than describing the total source rectangle as solid stone coverage.

Every body is closed. Concave source outlines use an ear-clipped top and bottom, with one interior microheight sample per cap triangle. This replaces the V12 radial-ring assumption and keeps the budget below 160,000 triangles. The nominal perimeter sampling is 190 mm, with extra authored corners preserved; the underlying bed is sampled at 205 mm. Broad edges are approximately 32–52 mm before miter compensation; tight fragments adapt their inset when needed to remain inside their source outline. Large surfaces retain bounded 2.8 mm scan residual around independently varied planes. Vertical scale is art direction, not metric source scan height.

Original Paving V10/V11/V12 and original downloaded maps are read-only. `build_hybrid_v13.py` is the canonical Blender builder; `prepare_v13.py` records initial seed/script scaffolding and must not overwrite the subsequently refined canonical builder. All source UVs are exported explicitly, colors remain white, and native face-cross/normal agreement must be positive. Independent QA also checks closed-body topology, signed volumes, projected top area versus footprint, exact source UV, original hashes and world bounds.

Native camera/water/shader integration remains root's work. Blender before/after cameras match each other; as documented in V12, their projection convention is not certified as a pixel-identical Unity camera. No new render starts while root FPS or another agent's Blender slot is active.
